    Help

    Choose a position and its replacement

    The other original engines stay as they are. Continue when your selection is ready, then choose the official firmware file. Your file stays in this browser.

    OUT selects the engine’s main sound. AUX selects its alternative sound instead. This changes the sound heard from that position through the MicroFreak’s usual audio output.

    Empty removes the sound from an original position to free space for your replacements. That position will be silent in the new firmware. Only positions you explicitly choose are changed.
