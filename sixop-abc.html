Your configuration

  • Two Op. FM → Six-Op A
  • Formant → Six-Op B
  • Speech → Six-Op C

All three are included together in this configuration. The other engines stay unchanged.

Type chooses A, B or C. Wave selects a patch; Timbre controls brightness; Shape controls the envelope. Hold Shift with Type, Wave, Timbre or Shape for Algorithm, Feedback, Detune or Release.

Presets from earlier Six-Op versions require selecting the bank and patch again and saving a copy. The DX LFO and second internal voice are omitted; MicroFreak's four voices are preserved.

Experimental update, checked in emulation. Physical testing of this exact firmware is pending.
